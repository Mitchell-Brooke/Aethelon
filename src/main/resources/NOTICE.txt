Aethelon - Third-party software notices
=======================================

The Aethelon mod itself is MIT licensed (see LICENSE).
Aethelon bundles several third-party Fabric mods inside its own jar via
jar-in-jar so that a single file installs the whole pack (plus two optional
mods that are installed separately because their licenses do not permit
redistribution, see the "Not bundled" section below).

None of the third-party binaries are committed to this repository. They are
downloaded at build time from the Modrinth CDN and pinned by SHA-512 in
build.gradle.
